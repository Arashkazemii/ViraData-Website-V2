# ViraData visual direction

Reading this as a premium B2B data and AI consultancy for business decision makers: clear, confident, and calm, with editorial spacing and purposeful motion.

- Mode: visual overhaul. Preserve routes, bilingual content, brand mark, demo disclosures, and metadata. The approved contact form omits the request-topic field.
- Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 7, MOTION_INTENSITY 7, VISUAL_DENSITY 3. Use asymmetry and generous spacing; keep motion secondary to reading.
- Foundation: existing Next.js, native CSS, and Motion. No new styling framework.
- Palette: deep navy `#0b1018`, layered slate surfaces, silver text, blue `#a8c8ff` accent. Keep one dark theme throughout. Semantic chart colors describe gains and losses.
- Type: self-hosted Manrope for English, licensed IRANYekanX for Persian. Preserve Persian word shaping and RTL reading order.
- Shape: 20px feature panels, 12px product panels, 8px inputs, pill controls and actions.
- Motion: word entrances establish hierarchy; scroll scenes explain the sample case; reveal transitions guide reading; pointer feedback acknowledges actions. React Bits Threads supplies the hero's flowing signal. Stop continuous work offscreen and in hidden tabs. Honor live reduced-motion changes. There is no manual motion pause control.
- Baseline: dark navy, Inter/IRANYekanX, square controls, alternating light sections, a real interactive synthetic-data report. Retain the useful report, replace abrupt palette shifts and dense visual rhythm.
